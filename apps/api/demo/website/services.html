Services and pricing

Garden redesign

Complete redesign of front or back gardens: layout, planting plans, paths and patios. Every redesign starts with an on-site garden design consultation for $150. Full redesign projects are quoted after the consultation.

Lawn maintenance

Weekly mowing, edging, blowing and weed control from April to October. Weekly lawn maintenance costs $45 to $85 per visit depending on lot size.

Irrigation repair

Sprinkler diagnostics, head and valve replacement and drip conversions. Most irrigation repairs cost between $120 and $400.

Seasonal cleanup

Spring or fall cleanup including leaves, pruning, bed refresh and haul-away for a flat $295.

Landscape installation

Patios, xeriscaping, planting and hardscape. Installation is priced individually after a site visit.
